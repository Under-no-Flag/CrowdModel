# 无量纲数字与原始仿真量可读性修改指南

**适用稿件**：*Macroscopic Crowd Control with Direction Constraints and Entrance-Rate Control for Urban Pedestrian Zones*  
**对应审稿意见**：2.8 处理“大量无量纲数字缺乏可读性”问题  
**修改目标**：让稿件中的标准化目标值、原始累计量、通道份额、入口限流量等数字具备明确的定义、单位、比较基准和物理解释。

---

## 0. 总体修改原则

当前稿件中出现了多类数字：

- 标准化目标值：如 `2.6338`、`0.5102`、`1.6851`、`0.7410`、`1.0140`；
- 通道流量份额：如 `48.78%`、`100.00%`、`82.30%`、`91.34%`；
- 原始仿真累计量：如 `415.75`、`1213.76`、`427.89`、`3239.295`、`2545.508`；
- 入口流率或容量上界：如 `0.2474`、`0.6000`、`0.1688`；
- 密度诊断量：如 `Peak density maximum ≈ 4.0`、`Average mean density = 0.003470`。

这些数字本身并非错误。问题在于：**读者不知道它们的量纲、标准化基准、分母、工程含义和可比范围**。因此修改时应遵循四个原则：

1. **首次出现必须定义**：每个标准化指标、raw count、flow、mass、cell-time 首次出现时给出定义式或表注说明。
2. **能给比例就给比例**：优先报告相对改进百分比、占总流入比例、占总通道流量比例，而不是只报告裸数。
3. **区分完整目标与诊断目标**：完整五项目标 `J`、三项诊断目标 `J^{(3)}`、分项标准化指标 `\tilde J_k` 不能混用。
4. **减少无意义小数位**：raw cumulative values 保留 2–3 位有效数字即可；标准化目标表中可保留 4 位小数。

---

## 1. 摘要部分：强调“标准化加权目标”，避免裸目标值

### 1.1 修改位置

**Abstract 最后实验结果段**。当前摘要已经不再直接突出 `2.6338`，而是使用“约 4.0% 改进”。这一方向是正确的，但建议进一步明确该目标是无量纲标准化目标。

### 1.2 建议替换文本

将摘要中的：

```text
Under a composite objective formed by weighted indicators including total travel time,
high-density exposure, and realized channel-load imbalance, repeated experiments show
that HCMBO achieves the lowest mean objective value.
```

替换为：

```text
Under a dimensionless composite objective obtained by weighting normalized travel-time,
high-density-exposure, channel-load-imbalance, waiting, and control-smoothness terms,
repeated experiments show that HCMBO achieves the lowest mean objective value.
```

### 1.3 修改理由

这样可以避免读者误以为 `objective` 是旅行时间、密度暴露或通道流量的物理单位量。摘要中应以“约 4.0% 相对改进”为主，而不是突出 `2.6338` 这种无量纲裸数。

## 2. Section III-F：补充标准化公式，这是最高优先级修改

### 2.1 修改位置

**Section III-F, Evaluation Metrics and Optimization Objective**。当前稿件已经定义了原始指标：

- `J_1`：总旅行时间；
- `J_2`：高密度暴露；
- `J_3`：通道负载不均衡；
- `J_4`：入口等待；
- `J_5`：控制平滑性。

但目前只写：

```text
Because the evaluation terms have different units and magnitudes, they are standardized before weighting:
\tilde J_1, \tilde J_2, \tilde J_3, \tilde J_4, \tilde J_5.
```

这不够。必须写出标准化公式。

### 2.2 建议小节标题

将：

```text
3) Standardized Scalar Objective
```

改为：

```text
3) Normalization and Scalar Objectives
```

### 2.3 建议插入的 LaTeX 文本

```latex
3) Normalization and Scalar Objectives:
The raw metrics in (18)--(23) have different physical units and numerical scales.
Therefore, all scalar objectives reported in the following experiments are computed
from normalized quantities.

Let
\[
M_{\rm ref}=M_{\rm init}+M_{\rm in},
\]
where \(M_{\rm init}\) is the initial crowd mass and \(M_{\rm in}\) is the
cumulative external inflow during \([0,T]\). Let \(|\Omega_w|\) be the walkable
area and \(R_{\rm tot}=\sum_{c=1}^{C}R_c(T)\) be the total realized channel
throughput. The normalized terms are defined as
\[
\tilde J_1(z)=\frac{J_1(z)}{M_{\rm ref}T},
\tag{24a}
\]
\[
\tilde J_2(z)=\frac{J_2(z)}{|\Omega_w|T},
\tag{24b}
\]
\[
\tilde J_3(z)=
\frac{\operatorname{Var}(R_1(T),\ldots,R_C(T))}
{R_{\rm tot}^2(C-1)/C^2+\varepsilon_R},
\tag{24c}
\]
\[
\tilde J_4(z)=\frac{J_4(z)}{M_{\rm ref}T},
\tag{24d}
\]
and
\[
\tilde J_5(z)=
\frac{J_5(z)}
{\sum_{c=1}^{C}\sum_{k=1}^{K-1}
\left[(\bar q_{c,k}^{+})^2+(\bar q_{c,k}^{-})^2\right]+\varepsilon_q}.
\tag{24e}
\]
Here \(\varepsilon_R\) and \(\varepsilon_q\) are small constants used only to avoid
zero denominators. With this normalization, \(\tilde J_1\) represents a relative
system-residence measure, \(\tilde J_2\) represents the fraction of walkable
space-time exposed to density above \(\rho_{\rm safe}\), and \(\tilde J_3\) is
zero for perfectly balanced channel use and approaches one when all realized
channel throughput concentrates on a single channel.
```

然后接完整目标：

```latex
Given weights \(\lambda=(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_5)\), the full scalar objective is
\[
J(z)=\sum_{k=1}^{5}\lambda_k\tilde J_k(z).
\tag{25}
\]
```

### 2.4 注意事项

如果代码中实际采用的是其他标准化方式，例如基于 reference case：

```latex
\tilde J_k(z)=\frac{J_k(z)}{J_k^{\rm ref}+\varepsilon_k},
```

则必须以代码真实实现为准，不要强行使用上面的公式。核心要求是：**不要只写“standardized”，而要写“how standardized”。**

## 3. Section III-F 后：补充“物理锚点”解释

### 3.1 修改位置

放在标准化公式之后、完整目标 `J` 之前或之后均可。

### 3.2 建议插入文本

```latex
Although \(J\), \(J^{(3)}\), and \(\tilde J_k\) are dimensionless, we keep their
physical interpretation through the normalization anchors. For example,
\(J_1/M_{\rm ref}\) can be interpreted as an average system residence time in
simulation time units; \(\tilde J_2\times 100\%\) is the percentage of the
walkable space-time domain exposed to density above \(\rho_{\rm safe}\); and
\(\tilde J_3\) measures the degree of realized channel-load concentration.
Therefore, scalar objectives are used only for ranking under a fixed weight
setting, whereas raw or ratio-based quantities are reported when physical
interpretation is needed.
```

### 3.3 修改效果

这一段能让读者理解：

- `\tilde J_2=0.00785` 可以解释为约 `0.785%` 的可通行时空域处于高密度暴露状态；
- `J=2.6338` 是固定权重下的管理评分，不是秒、人数或密度；
- `J^{(3)}` 是诊断评分，不等同于完整优化目标。

## 6. Section IV-B：定义 reverse-direction mass time 和 counterflow cell time

### 6.1 修改位置

**Section IV-B Mechanism Validation**。当前正文写：

```text
... reverse-direction mass time, and counterflow cell time from 73.18%, 415.75, and 1213.76 to zero.
```

### 6.2 建议插入定义

在第一次出现 “reverse-direction mass time” 之前插入：

```latex
For the bidirectional validation case, we use two diagnostic quantities.
The reverse-direction mass time is defined as
\[
{\rm RMT} =
\sum_{n}\sum_{i\in\Omega_c}
\rho_i^n
\mathbf 1\{u_i^n \cdot \tau_c <0\}
\Delta x^2\Delta t ,
\]
which measures the cumulative amount of mass moving against the prescribed
channel direction. The counterflow cell time is defined as
\[
{\rm CCT} =
\sum_n \sum_{i\in\Omega_c}
\mathbf 1\{\rho_{i,+}^n>0,\rho_{i,-}^n>0\}\Delta t ,

which counts the cumulative grid-cell time during which opposite-direction
subpopulations coexist in the same channel region.
```

### 6.3 建议替换原句

将原句改为：

```latex
In the baseline bidirectional inflow case, the westbound middle-channel share,
RMT, and CCT are 73.18\%, 415.75 mass-time units, and 1213.76 cell-time units,
respectively. After imposing the eastbound one-way rule, all three quantities
are reduced to zero, corresponding to complete elimination of reverse flow and
local counterflow within the controlled channel.
```
